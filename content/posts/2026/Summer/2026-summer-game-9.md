## First Half

The Frothers lined up with **Cory** in goal, **Chris** and **Ian** at centre back, and **Andy** and **Campbell** at full back. **Yarride**, **Vinh** and **Nikolai** made up the midfield, with **Jamie**, **Sammy** and **Farshid** up top. **Evan**, **Lance** and **Charles** were on the bench.

Between the sticks was **Cory**, playing big Ron Atkinson. The flashy one, never happier than when he's showing off, and he wasn't going to stay in goal all day.

Marshalling the back line was **Chris**, playing Erik ten Hag. The ego has landed. Don't question the decisions, or he'll flash that one time he did one good thing at you.

Next to him at centre back, **Ian** played Louis van Gaal, a man of few words and intense stares.

Up top was **Farshid**, playing José Mourinho. Unquestionably majestic when he's there and switched in, but the red mist can descend, and he can become straight despondent.

Alongside him was **Sammy**, playing Ole Gunnar Solskjær. The baby face was absent, so it was left to the skinny legs to do the talking.

It was **Jamie** who got us underway. The Frothers sprang the offside trap, **Jamie** was through on his own, and he finished calmly past the keeper. It was his first goal back in Frothers colours, and great to see him back in the line-up.

> 1-0

The opposition levelled when we lost the ball in midfield and their talisman, number 24 **Michael Jordan**, was given far too much space to have a hit. He put it hard and low past **Cory**.

> 1-1

The Frothers changed positions after that, with **Yarride** going up top, and it wasn't long before he found his way into the box. The ball fell to his left foot and he hit it hard enough to put the Frothers back in front.

> 2-1

From there it turned into the **Nikolai** show. **Ian** set him up for the third.

> 3-1

**Sammy** then got in on the act with one of his own.

> 4-1

**Nikolai** added another, with **Sammy** setting him up this time.

> 5-1

The sixth came from the penalty spot. **Sammy** made a lovely run into the box and was tripped up and pulled back, so the Frothers were awarded a penalty, which **Nikolai** stepped up to and finished strongly into the corner for his hat-trick.

> 6-1
