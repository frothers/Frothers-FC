The Frothers have recorded their biggest ever win, but this match report is in honour to another team setting records on the other side of the globe.

![](/2026-11-22/goldbridge.gif)

Manchester United have been getting through managers faster than the Frothers get through subs, and this week we pay homage to the fallen.

![](/2026-11-22/infantino.gif)

We'll meet each of the managers as the goals went in.

## First Half

It was **Jamie** who got us underway. Playing Ralf Rangnick, he's a man who's always flirting but never quite ready to commit to the cause, one foot out the door, but showing signs that he could really turn this club around. The Frothers sprang the offside trap, Jamie was through on his own, and he finished calmly past the keeper. It was his first goal back in Frothers colours, and great to see him back in the line-up.

> 1-0

![](/2026-11-22/rangnick.gif)

The opposition levelled when we lost the ball in midfield and their talisman, number 24 **Michael Jordan**, was given far too much space to have a hit. He put it hard and low to tie it up.

> 1-1

![](/2026-11-22/jordan.gif)

The Frothers changed positions after that, with **Yarride** going up top, and it wasn't long before he found his way into the box. Yarride played Ruben Amorim, ultimately getting paid regardless of the result, and smiling widest when the red cards come out. The ball fell to his left foot and he hit it hard enough to put the Frothers back in front.

> 2-1

![](/2026-11-22/amorim.gif)

From there it turned into the **Nikolai** show. Nikolai was Sir Matt Busby, the man with the babes. Nikolai's first was a cross too pure for this world, which ended up just nestling in the top corner.

![](/2026-11-22/pepe-plane.gif)

Seriously though, don't you put that evil on me Ricky Bobby.

> 3-1

**Sam** then got in on the act with one of his own, set up by Nikolai. He played Ole Gunnar Solskjær. The baby face was at the forefront this week, having had a recent shave.

![](/2026-11-22/ole.gif)

> 4-1

Nikolai added another, with Sam setting him up this time.

> 5-1

The sixth came from the penalty spot. Sam made a lovely run into the box and was pulled back, so the Frothers were awarded a penalty, which Nikolai stepped up to and finished strongly into the corner for his hat-trick.

> 6-1

Behind the goal rush, **Campbell** played Ryan Giggs at full back, here for a good time, with unrestrained sexual energy, but relegated to the chair in the corner this match.

**Vinh** completed the midfield as Michael Carrick. He's been around the club for a while, but he's finally been brought in in an official capacity. He meshes well with the boys, but a few of them have started talking shit behind his back.

## Second Half

It was time to unleash the ultimate weapon: unchained **Andy**. Playing Ruud van Nistelrooy, he's always kept at the back of the bench, never brought to the front, but when he does sneak forward, he excels. The pure presence of the man makes back lines look like they're staging a dressing room revolution. Two quick goals started the second half on a dangerous trajectory.

> 7-1

> 8-1

United then pulled one back. **Michael Jordan** got to play the isolation game and, smelling some fresh fish at the back, decided to break some ankles.

![](/2026-11-22/marge.gif)

> 8-2

**Farshid** then got up the pitch and delivered a corner. Farshid played José Mourinho, unquestionably majestic when he's there and switched in, but the red mist can descend, and he can become straight up despondent.

The keeper could only spill the corner to a seagulling **Chris**, who had just gone up top, and he gratefully knocked it in. Chris was Erik ten Hag. The ego has landed. Don't question the decisions, or he'll flash that one time he did one good thing at you.

> 9-2

Farshid then wet his own whistle, running up from the back to pick some ripe fruit, courtesy of a pass from Ian. It was a decision that got him relegated back to goalie.

**Ian** was playing David Moyes. Every time you think Ian's washed and done, he shoots out another glorious performance that has you hanging on his every move for the next six months and calling him the Ianfestation.

> 10-2

Almost instantly after **Farshid** went in goal, United got another, chipping a gloveless Farshid.

> 10-3

Off the bench came **Charles**, playing Mauricio Pochettino. He's got the slick locks and that American appeal.

**Evan** followed, playing Louis van Gaal, a man of few words and intense stares, and unquestionably effective in most teams.

It was then the Statler and Waldorf show up top, with Chris and Charles linking up for all the goal contributions, with Ian shouldering his way in on a technicality for the assist on the first.

![](/2026-11-22/statler-waldorf.gif)

> 11-3

> 12-3

> 13-3

Then **Lance** was put on up top, playing Sir Alex Ferguson, the club GOAT, and nobody quite knows what we're going to do when he leaves. Just don't get him angry. He teed up Cory for his first Frothers goal.

**Cory** was playing big Ron Atkinson, the flashy one, never happier than when he's showing off, and he wasn't going to stay in goal all day.

> 14-3

Then, with the hairdryer kept firmly in the kit bag, Lance selflessly scored himself rather than letting Charles get a hat-trick off the toe.

> 15-3

## Man of the Match
**Jamie** gets it, the impact maker, for the first goal and for his tireless work in the middle of the park.

A mention too for **Josh**, who kept the kids busy on the sidelines for the whole game while injured, even with sabotage coming from on the pitch.

## Dick of the Day
**Ian** takes it, for hitting **Charles**' sweet, innocent daughter in the face with a no-pressure clearance, causing her to spill her raisins and start crying. A big fine is coming.
