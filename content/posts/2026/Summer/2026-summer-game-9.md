Manchester United have been getting through managers faster than the Frothers get through subs, and a team that could definitely do with a few goals these days turned up this week to face a Frothers squad with a manager of its own for every player. By full time the Frothers had put 15 goals past United, equalling the biggest haul of their career, with the Red Devils' managerial chaos on display throughout. Rather than a team talk, we'll meet each of the managers as the goals went in.

## First Half

The Frothers lined up with **Cory** in goal, **Chris** and **Ian** at centre back, and **Andy** and **Campbell** at full back. **Yarride**, **Vinh** and **Nikolai** made up the midfield, with **Jamie**, **Sammy** and **Farshid** up top. **Evan**, **Lance** and **Charles** were on the bench.

It was **Jamie** who got us underway. Playing Ralf Rangnick, he's a man who's always flirting but never quite ready to commit to the cause, one foot out the door, but showing signs that he could really turn this club around. The Frothers sprang the offside trap, Jamie was through on his own, and he finished calmly past the keeper. It was his first goal back in Frothers colours, and great to see him back in the line-up.

> 1-0

The opposition levelled when we lost the ball in midfield and their talisman, number 24 **Michael Jordan**, was given far too much space to have a hit. He put it hard and low past **Cory**, who was playing big Ron Atkinson. The flashy one, never happier than when he's showing off, and he wasn't going to stay in goal all day.

> 1-1

The Frothers changed positions after that, with **Yarride** going up top, and it wasn't long before he found his way into the box. Yarride played Ruben Amorim, ultimately getting paid regardless of the result, and smiling widest when the red cards come out. The ball fell to his left foot and he hit it hard enough to put the Frothers back in front.

> 2-1

From there it turned into the **Nikolai** show. Nikolai was Sir Matt Busby, the man with the babes, and as it turned out, the man to make a mess of the other side. **Ian**, playing David Moyes, set him up for the third. Every time you think Ian's washed and done, he shoots out another glorious performance that has you hanging on his every move for the next six months. Nikolai's first was a cross too pure for this world, which ended up just nestling in the top corner.

> 3-1

**Sammy** then got in on the act with one of his own, set up by Nikolai. He played Ole Gunnar Solskjær. The baby face was absent, so it was left to the skinny legs to do the talking.

> 4-1

Nikolai added another, with Sammy setting him up this time.

> 5-1

The sixth came from the penalty spot. Sammy made a lovely run into the box and was tripped up and pulled back, so the Frothers were awarded a penalty, which Nikolai stepped up to and finished strongly into the corner for his hat-trick.

> 6-1

Behind the goal rush, **Campbell** played Ryan Giggs at full back, here for a good time, with unrestrained sexual energy, while **Vinh** completed the midfield as Michael Carrick. He's been around the club for a while, but he's finally been brought in in an official capacity. He meshes well with the boys, but suddenly one of them starts talking shit behind his back.

## Second Half

It was time to unleash the ultimate weapon: unchained **Andy**. Playing Ruud van Nistelrooy, he's always kept at the back, never brought to the front, but when he does sneak forward, he excels. The pure presence of the man makes back lines look like they're staging a dressing room revolution. Two quick goals started the second half on a dangerous trajectory, the first set up by **Yarride** and the second by Ian.

> 7-1

> 8-1

**Farshid** then got up the pitch and delivered a corner. Farshid played José Mourinho, unquestionably majestic when he's there and switched in, but the red mist can descend, and he can become straight despondent. The keeper could only spill the corner to a seagulling **Chris**, who had just gone up top, and he gratefully knocked it in. Chris was Erik ten Hag. The ego has landed. Don't question the decisions, or he'll flash that one time he did one good thing at you.

> 9-1

Farshid then wet his own whistle, running up from the back to pick some ripe fruit, courtesy of a pass from Ian. It was a decision that got him relegated back to goalie.

> 10-1

Off the bench came **Charles**, playing Mauricio Pochettino. He never did get the United job, despite being linked with it for years, but he's got the slick locks and that American appeal. **Evan** followed, playing Louis van Gaal, a man of few words and intense stares, and unquestionably effective in most teams.

It was then the Statler and Waldorf show up top, with Chris and Charles linking up for all the goal contributions, with Ian shouldering his way in on a technicality for the assist on the first.

> 11-1

> 12-1

> 13-1

Then **Lance** was put on up top, playing Sir Alex Ferguson, the club GOAT, and nobody quite knows what we're going to do when he leaves. Just don't get him angry. He teed up Cory for his first Frothers goal.

> 14-1

Then, with the hairdryer kept firmly in the kit bag, he selflessly scored himself rather than letting Charles get a hat-trick off the toe.

> 15-1

## Man of the Match
**Jamie** gets it, the impact maker, for the first goal and for his tireless work in the middle of the park.

A mention too for **Josh**, who kept the kids busy on the sidelines for the whole game while injured, even with sabotage coming from on the pitch.

## Dick of the Day
**Ian** takes it, for hitting **Charles**' sweet, innocent daughter in the face with a no-pressure clearance, causing her to spill her raisins and start crying. A big fine is coming.
