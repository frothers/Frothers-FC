Back to Ascot Park for the Sunday game, this time against Inter Wellington, a side with a reputation for being rather dirty, who have been through a whole bunch of team names over the years. They also have a target man up top in Marco, otherwise known as the Chinese Ronaldo.

## First Half

It was very windy at Ascot Park. The game kicked off with plenty of attack down the left wing, but as soon as play switched to the right, the Frothers were straight in. **Nikolai** beat a couple of defenders, knocked it forward and ran after it, then delivered a pinpoint cross for **Sammy** to tap home.

> 1-0

The Frothers were very glad to be in front, and the half progressed with a taste of their own medicine. The Inter Wellington centre back came bumbling up the pitch, somehow found his way into a darting run down the right, and put in a great cross for the back post, where Marco came in and tapped it in to level things up.

> 1-1

Marco again next time, finding space on the edge of the box and leathering it into the corner to put Inter Wellington in front.

> 1-2

The rest of the half played out without a whole lot more opportunities. **Cory** came steaming out of goal on multiple occasions to intercept the ball, once getting a bit too excited and taking it all the way to the sideline, where he had to rush the throw-in taker to get back to goal in time.

## Second Half

With the wind now at their backs, it didn't take the Frothers long. From the very first goal kick, **Chris** honked one long, the ball got nudged on and ran down to the byline, and it was squared up for **Yarride**. He was able to take as many touches as he wanted in the box, stop, have a look, and then calmly put it into the side of the goalkeeper to get the Frothers back level.

> 2-2

A few good corners followed, but the best one came when **Yarride** put one in and found **Charles**, who had more space and time than he knew what to do with, and managed to bump the ball into the net. We won't talk about the specifics.

> 3-2

The Frothers were not helped by a bench that dwindled from three subs, one of whom was on kids' duty in the playground, to none at all, with **Farshid** and **Andy** managing to disappear off the radar entirely. So when **Nikolai** picked up an injury, there was nobody to bring on, and the Frothers had to play a significant portion of the game with ten men.

It was still all Frothers for the majority of the game, but Inter Wellington put the foot down at the end, threw caution to the wind and started pressing hard. Those ten men certainly played a part in what came next. They eked out a penalty when their attacker sneakily put his foot in front of a clearing boot to take the hit. It came up to Marco for his hat-trick, but he could only find the upright, and it bounced out to preserve the Frothers' 3-2 lead.

That's how the game ended, the Frothers continuing their winning run. There was a bit of argy-bargy during the match, of course, as the opposition are known for, but it was actually the Frothers who managed to pick up most of the cards, with some back chat to the referee combined with some pretty nasty professional fouls for a Div 4 league.

## Man of the Match
**Charles** gets it, coming on and bagging the winner as well as not conceding a goal while he was on the field. There's not much more you can ask from a centre back.

## Dick of the Day
It has to be my boy **Farshid**, and **Andy** too, for taking the Frothers from three subs to none at all by just disappearing off the radar. When **Nikolai** got injured, we had to go down to ten men for a significant portion of play, and we can blame that for the penalty conceded.
